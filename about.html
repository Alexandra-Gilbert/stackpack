About Stackpack

We believe vendor management should be effortless, transparent, and powered by intelligence — not spreadsheets.

Stackpack was born to give finance and operations teams back their time. Our AI works silently in the background, connecting all your tools and contracts, so you always have a complete view of your vendor ecosystem. No more hunting for renewal dates, no more duplicate subscriptions, and no more surprises.

Alexandra Gilbert

Founder & CEO

Former fintech executive with 15+ years in corporate finance and supply chain management. Alexandra started Stackpack after witnessing how much time finance teams wasted on manual vendor tracking — and how much money they lost due to missed renewal opportunities.
